## 2. Official sources

### NPC Circular No. 2022-04

Official source:
https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04.pdf

Section 5 identifies mandatory-registration conditions including a PIC/PIP that:
- employs **250 or more persons**;
- processes sensitive personal information of **1,000 or more individuals**;
- processes data likely to pose a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects.

The Circular also provides a specific rule for a Data Processing System involving automated decision-making or profiling.

### Sensitive-information relevance in the current product design

The DPA/NPC definition includes categories such as:
- age/marital status;
- health;
- education;
- government-issued records peculiar to an individual, including licences and related status;
- other information classified by law.

Official NPC definition:
https://privacy.gov.ph/glossary-of-data-privacy-terms/

Business & Life current design can process data relevant to this classification, including:
- private Service Provider CV / qualification evidence that may contain education data;
- PRC or other government-issued licence evidence where a regulated category requires it;
- Courier/governed-profile eligibility evidence where government credentials apply;
- support/incident evidence that may contain sensitive data supplied for a legitimate case;
- health-related information if users actually supply/process it through supported personal-money/support contexts.

This does **not** mean every CV, transaction or support ticket is sensitive personal information.

Required before final decision:
- identify each launch data field/document actually collected;
- classify it;
- determine how many unique data subjects' SPI will be processed;
- monitor the threshold after launch if initial registration does not proceed on another trigger.

# Philippines Pilot & Expansion Master Roadmap

## Principle

Do not wait for every future global feature before proving one reliable local economic loop.

Do not expand because registration count looks impressive.

Expand only when the current territory can complete real economic activity reliably and its operations can be reproduced from documented procedures rather than individual memory.

## Gate model

Every launch item uses:

- READY — evidence exists and acceptance is satisfied;
- HOLD — known blocker;
- UNKNOWN — evidence missing.

UNKNOWN is not READY.

## Phase 0 — Architecture / governance baseline

- product architecture;
- public/private documentation;
- profile governance model;
- business tenancy;
- country edition;
- risk/security baseline.

- security/backup;
- compliance;
- documentation;

## Phase 11 — Next territory

Before activating another territory, produce:
- territory launch pack;
- appointed Territory Admin;
- local Merchant pipeline;
- Courier capacity;
- Supplier/service availability plan;
- local compliance review;
- authority relationship pack;
- support capacity;
- success metrics baseline.

## Phase 12 — Country replication

Only after Philippines operating model is repeatable:
- isolate new country edition;
- perform legal/payment research;
- appoint country leadership;
- translate/localize documentation;
- create country launch gates;
- start one controlled territory.

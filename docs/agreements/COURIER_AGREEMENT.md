# Delivery Provider Participation Agreement Framework

> Controlled draft. Requires legal review before final acceptance language. This agreement defines platform-role obligations; it does not by itself establish employment, agency, franchise, partnership or independent-contractor legal status.

## Purpose

Define platform obligations for a Delivery Provider/Courier performing approved assignments.

## Participant obligations

- Maintain truthful identity/vehicle/eligibility evidence.
- Operate only while platform eligibility is approved/current.
- Use Availability truthfully.
- Follow assigned route/status workflow.
- Protect Customer location/contact/handoff code.
- Share location only for active delivery purpose.
- Report safety/incidents promptly.

## Prohibited conduct

- Operate while suspended/expired/unapproved.
- Falsify pickup/delivery/completion.
- Request handoff code before real handoff.
- Share Customer data/location improperly.
- Accept unauthorized cash custody.
- Misrepresent platform approval as government transport authority.

## Platform / legal boundaries

- Legal worker/contractor/employment status is not defined by this agreement framework.
- Vehicle/licence/insurance/transport requirements depend on actual model and verified rules.
- LTFRB applicability to goods-delivery cannot be assumed without specific analysis.
- Compensation/fees require final commercial/legal model.

## Required controlled documents

The participant should have access to:
- applicable profile playbook;
- current SOPs;
- compliance requirement checklist;
- Privacy Notice;
- Code of Conduct;
- applicable commercial terms when activated.

## Authorization

Profile access remains subject to current application/authorization state and may be suspended/revoked/expire under documented policy.

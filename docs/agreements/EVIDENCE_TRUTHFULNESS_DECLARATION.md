# Truthfulness of Evidence Declaration

I declare that, to the best of my knowledge:

1. the documents/information I submit are authentic and relate to me/my authorized business/profile;
2. I have not knowingly altered evidence to mislead Business & Life;
3. dates, reference numbers and issuer information I provide are accurate;
4. I will notify/update Business & Life when required evidence expires, is revoked or materially changes;
5. platform verification does not turn the document into government-issued evidence;
6. false evidence may result in review, suspension/revocation and further action according to applicable policy/law.

This declaration does not replace any sworn/notarized/government declaration required by law.

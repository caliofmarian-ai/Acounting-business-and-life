# Merchant Role Agreement Framework

> Controlled draft. Requires legal review before final acceptance language. This agreement defines platform-role obligations; it does not by itself establish employment, agency, franchise, partnership or independent-contractor legal status.

## Purpose

Define the Business & Life platform obligations of an approved Merchant operating a business/storefront.

## Participant obligations

- Maintain accurate business/storefront/product information.
- Process Customer orders truthfully.
- Record real payments and outstanding balances accurately.
- Maintain reasonable stock/pricing information.
- Protect Customer/business data.
- Keep required business/permit evidence current.
- Use Supplier/Delivery workflows consistently.
- Comply with lawful/product restrictions and current platform policies.

## Prohibited conduct

- Sell prohibited/illegal goods/services.
- Falsify payments/sales/permits.
- Access another business ledger.
- Manipulate stock/accounting/audit to conceal discrepancies.
- Misrepresent Business & Life approval as government authorization.
- Misuse Customer data.

## Platform / legal boundaries

- Merchant remains responsible for its own business registrations/tax/permits.
- Business & Life platform approval is separate from government authorization.
- Final seller/platform/consumer relationship terms require RA 11967/IRR review.
- Fees are not assumed until approved/disclosed.

## Required controlled documents

The participant should have access to:
- applicable profile playbook;
- current SOPs;
- compliance requirement checklist;
- Privacy Notice;
- Code of Conduct;
- applicable commercial terms when activated.

## Authorization

Profile access remains subject to current application/authorization state and may be suspended/revoked/expire under documented policy.

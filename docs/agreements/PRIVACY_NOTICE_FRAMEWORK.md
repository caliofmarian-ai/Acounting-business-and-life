# Business & Life — Privacy Notice Framework

Do not publish placeholders as facts.

## 2. What information the platform may process

Depending on profile/function:
- account identity/contact;
- profile/business details;
- orders/transactions;
- business accounting records;
- Supplier relationships/POs;
- Delivery status/location during active delivery;
- professional credentials/CV/portfolio;
- support/incident records;
- uploaded evidence;
- acknowledgements/consents;
- security/audit logs.

## 3. Purposes

Examples:
- create/secure account;
- deliver requested platform functions;
- process/record orders and business operations;
- authorize governed profiles;
- verify credentials/evidence;
- coordinate Delivery/Local Services;
- provide support;
- detect/manage abuse/incidents;
- maintain accounting/audit records;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

Every processing system requires a documented legal/purpose basis before EFFECTIVE policy publication.

## 4. Data minimization

Collect/use only information reasonably necessary for defined purpose.

Examples:
- Courier live location is purpose-limited to active delivery.
- Private credential/CV evidence is separate from public profile.
- Incident evidence remains private.
- Analytics should avoid unnecessary raw PII.

## 5. Who may receive/access data

Possible recipients depending on purpose:
- account user;
- authorized business members;
- counterparties necessary for an order/job;
- authorized platform support/admin/reviewer;
- service providers/processors;
- government/authority where legally required/authorized.

Final recipient/processor list must reflect actual integrations/vendors.

## 6. Public vs private information

Public may include intentionally published:
- Merchant storefront/product;
- public Service Provider profile/services/selected credential status;
- public ratings where enabled.

Private includes purpose-limited:
- business ledgers;
- private CV/evidence;
- incident attachments;
- admin review details;
- private generated documents;
- security credentials/tokens.

## 7. Retention

Retention periods must be defined by record type, legal/business need and security/privacy policy.

Do not state arbitrary retention periods until approved.

## 8. Data subject rights

Final notice must explain applicable rights/process under Philippine privacy law and how requests are submitted/verified.

A technical authenticated request channel is implemented through `Help -> New issue -> Privacy & data rights`. It may be referenced only as a submission channel; it does not replace the need to identify the actual controller/privacy contact or define the approved handling/verification process.

## 9. Security

Describe implemented safeguards accurately without guaranteeing absolute security.

## 10. International / cross-border processing

Assess and disclose actual hosting/processors/data flows before EFFECTIVE publication.

## 11. Children/minors

If the platform permits or processes minor data, define a verified policy/consent basis before launch. Do not infer one here.

## 12. Changes/contact

Maintain version/effective date and contact method.

## Pre-publication checklist

- actual PIC/PIP/operator assessment;
- DPO/NPCRS applicability assessment;
- vendor/processor register;
- processing inventory/legal-basis map;
- retention schedule;
- data-subject rights workflow;
- security/breach procedure;
- review against DPA/IRR/NPC guidance.

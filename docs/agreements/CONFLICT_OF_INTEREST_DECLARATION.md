# Conflict of Interest Declaration

## Purpose

Protect approval, verification, procurement, partnership and enforcement decisions from undisclosed personal interests.

## Declare where applicable

- close family/personal relationship with applicant/participant;
- financial/business ownership or debt;
- gifts/benefits;
- outside employment/consulting;
- supplier/partner interests;
- dispute/adverse relationship that may affect neutrality.

## Required action

If a conflict may affect a decision:
1. disclose it promptly;
2. do not hide or destroy evidence;
3. recuse/seek reassignment where policy requires;
4. record the resolution/authorized exception.

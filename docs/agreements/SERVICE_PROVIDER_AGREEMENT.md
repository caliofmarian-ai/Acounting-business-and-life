# Local Services Provider Agreement Framework

> Controlled draft. Requires legal review before final acceptance language. This agreement defines platform-role obligations; it does not by itself establish employment, agency, franchise, partnership or independent-contractor legal status.

## Purpose

Define platform obligations for a Service Provider offering approved local skills/services.

## Participant obligations

- Maintain truthful experience/services/credential information.
- Operate only within authorized platform scope and applicable legal scope.
- Quote transparently.
- Perform accepted jobs professionally/safely.
- Protect Customer/property/privacy.
- Use portfolio content only with required consent.
- Mark completion truthfully.

## Prohibited conduct

- Falsify credentials/reviews/work completion.
- Offer prohibited/unauthorized regulated work.
- Publish Customer job content without consent.
- Use Customer data beyond job purpose.
- Misrepresent platform verification as government/professional licence.

## Platform / legal boundaries

- Professional/legal requirements depend on category and jurisdiction.
- PRC/TESDA evidence is category-specific, not universal.
- Employment/contractor status is not established by this framework.
- Payment/platform-fee terms require final commercial model.

## Required controlled documents

The participant should have access to:
- applicable profile playbook;
- current SOPs;
- compliance requirement checklist;
- Privacy Notice;
- Code of Conduct;
- applicable commercial terms when activated.

## Authorization

Profile access remains subject to current application/authorization state and may be suspended/revoked/expire under documented policy.

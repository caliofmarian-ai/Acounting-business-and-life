# Business & Life — Code of Conduct

## Principle

Use Business & Life honestly, safely, respectfully and only within your authorized role.

## Required conduct

All participants must:
- provide truthful information;
- treat users/participants respectfully;
- protect private/confidential information;
- follow applicable SOPs and profile obligations;
- record operational/payment status truthfully;
- declare/escalate conflicts or safety/compliance concerns;
- cooperate with legitimate incident investigations;
- keep required credentials/evidence current.

## Prohibited conduct

- fraud or deliberate misrepresentation;
- falsified credentials, permits, payments, deliveries or reviews;
- harassment/discrimination/threats;
- retaliation against good-faith reporter;
- unauthorized access/data use;
- sharing private evidence outside purpose;
- manipulation of accounting/stock/audit to conceal facts;
- bribery/improper inducement in approval decisions;
- false statements of government/platform authority;
- bypassing approval/safety/payment controls.

## Conflicts of interest

Privileged decision-makers must disclose personal/financial relationships that could affect:
- approval;
- verification;
- enforcement;
- procurement;
- partnership;
- financial decisions.

## Consequences

Potential platform actions, according to authority/policy:
- warning/training;
- restricted capability;
- suspension;
- revocation;
- escalation/investigation;
- referral to competent authority where appropriate/legally required.

Actions require reason/audit and applicable appeal/review process once defined.

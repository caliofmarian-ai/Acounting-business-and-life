# Supplier Role Agreement Framework

> Controlled draft. Requires legal review before final acceptance language. This agreement defines platform-role obligations; it does not by itself establish employment, agency, franchise, partnership or independent-contractor legal status.

## Purpose

Define the Business & Life platform obligations of an approved Supplier serving Merchant businesses.

## Participant obligations

- Maintain accurate catalog/availability/pack/pricing information.
- Accept relationships/POs only when genuinely intended.
- Confirm/fulfil quantities and prices truthfully.
- Record delivery/receiving/payment status accurately.
- Protect Merchant commercial/private information.
- Keep applicable business/product compliance evidence current.

## Prohibited conduct

- Falsify availability/delivery/payment.
- Alter historical PO facts to conceal changes.
- Use Merchant data outside relationship purpose.
- Misrepresent registration/licence/approval.
- Duplicate or manipulate linked accounting records.

## Platform / legal boundaries

- Supplier remains responsible for its own legal/business/product obligations.
- Merchant↔Supplier relationship is separate from profile approval.
- Commercial/payment/fee clauses require actual policy/provider review.

## Required controlled documents

The participant should have access to:
- applicable profile playbook;
- current SOPs;
- compliance requirement checklist;
- Privacy Notice;
- Code of Conduct;
- applicable commercial terms when activated.

## Authorization

Profile access remains subject to current application/authorization state and may be suspended/revoked/expire under documented policy.

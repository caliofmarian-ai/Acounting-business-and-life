# Business & Life — Platform Terms Framework

> Controlled draft. This is a legal-document framework for later review and publication. It is not represented as final legal advice or final enforceable Terms until the operating entity, commercial model and legal review are complete.

Do not publish invented operator details.

## 2. What the platform does

Business & Life provides software/workflows supporting local:
- Customer discovery/orders;
- Merchant operations/accounting;
- Supplier procurement;
- Delivery coordination;
- Local Services requests/jobs;
- role/profile authorization;
- support/incidents;
- documentation/compliance guidance.

The platform may add or retire features through versioned updates.

## 3. Account responsibilities

Users must:
- provide accurate account/profile information;
- protect credentials and account access;
- use only permissions granted to them;
- keep contact/evidence reasonably current where required;
- report suspected unauthorized access.

Users must not:
- impersonate others;
- bypass access restrictions;
- submit fraudulent evidence;
- misuse private data;
- interfere with service/security.

## 4. Profile-specific obligations

Additional controlled agreements may apply to:
- Merchant;
- Supplier;
- Delivery Provider;
- Service Provider;
- privileged Admin/operator roles.

Accepting general platform terms does not automatically authorize a governed operational profile.

## 5. Orders, payments and commercial records

Where applicable:
- prices/terms shown before order acceptance should be reviewed by the user;
- payment status records money actually confirmed/recorded through supported workflows;
- outstanding amounts may remain payable/receivable;
- refunds/settlements/fees follow current implemented and disclosed policy;
- client-side success must not be represented as authoritative payment confirmation when the payment architecture requires provider confirmation.

Final payment/refund/fee clauses require commercial/payment-provider legal review.

## 6. Marketplace / third-party participants

Merchant, Supplier, Courier and Service Provider profiles may be separate businesses/participants rather than the platform operator.

Final Terms must clearly define:
- who is seller/service provider;
- who contracts with whom;
- platform intermediary role;
- applicable consumer redress;
- prohibited goods/services;
- fee allocation;
- liability boundaries.

These details require review against the actual operating model and RA 11967/IRR.

## 7. Acceptable use

Prohibited:
- illegal goods/services;
- fraud/deception;
- false licences/credentials;
- harassment/threats;
- privacy invasion;
- manipulation of reviews/incidents;
- payment abuse;
- platform/security abuse.

Detailed prohibited-products policy must be separately maintained and legally sourced.

## 8. Evidence / verification

Platform verification:
- is a Business & Life control;
- does not automatically equal government licensing/certification;
- may expire/suspend/revoke according to documented evidence status.

## 9. Privacy

Personal information is handled according to the published Privacy Notice and applicable privacy law/policy.

## 10. Suspension / termination

The platform may restrict profile/account capability under controlled policy for reasons including:
- fraud/safety;
- expired/invalid evidence;
- unauthorized access;
- material policy breach;
- legal/regulatory requirement.

Final clauses must include notice/appeal/process appropriate to the role and law.

## 11. Intellectual property

Final Terms should define:
- Business & Life platform IP;
- user content licence needed to operate the service;
- Merchant/product/portfolio content rights;
- takedown/report process.

Do not claim ownership of user content beyond the rights actually required.

## 12. Changes to Terms

Version:
- terms version;
- effective date;
- material change summary;
- re-acceptance requirement where appropriate.

## 13. Governing law / dispute provisions

# Privileged Admin Access & Confidentiality Agreement Framework

> Controlled draft. Final employment/contractor/confidentiality clauses require legal review.

## Applies to

Super Admin, Country Admin, Territory Admin, Support, Compliance Reviewer and other privileged operators according to assigned scope.

## Privileged-access obligations

The holder agrees to:
- use admin access only for assigned Business & Life duties;
- follow least privilege and territory/country scope;
- access private evidence only when necessary;
- keep credentials/secrets confidential;
- record decisions/reasons through audited workflows;
- follow conflict-of-interest rules;
- report suspected security/privacy abuse immediately;
- return/remove access on role end/change.

## Strictly prohibited

- browsing private data out of curiosity;
- sharing/exporting evidence without purpose;
- changing money/accounting facts without valid workflow/evidence;
- approving related parties without declared conflict;
- accepting bribes/improper benefits;
- using admin authority for retaliation;
- bypassing audit/security controls;
- falsely representing government authority.

## Monitoring / audit

Privileged actions may be logged/audited subject to approved privacy/security policy.

## Role end

Access must be revoked/updated promptly on:
- termination;
- role change;
- suspension;
- loss of business need;
- security concern.

## Acknowledgement

Versioned acknowledgement is mandatory before privileged access is considered fully governed.

# Business & Life Philippines — Prohibited and Restricted Goods & Services Policy

## 1. Purpose

This controlled draft defines which goods, services and offers may not be published through the Philippines edition of Business & Life, which require additional review, and which are temporarily unsupported during the controlled pilot.

It is designed to protect Customers, Merchants, Suppliers, Service Providers, Couriers and the platform from illegal trade, unsafe commerce, fraud, exploitation and deceptive listings.

This document is not represented as final Philippine legal advice. It must receive legal/compliance review before becoming EFFECTIVE.

## 2. Policy states

Every governed catalogue/service category must resolve to one of these states before public publication:

- **PROHIBITED** — cannot be listed, offered, booked, sold or fulfilled through Business & Life.
- **RESTRICTED_REVIEW** — remains unpublished until required evidence and authorization are verified.
- **PILOT_HOLD** — Business & Life does not support this category during the controlled PH pilot, even where a lawful route may exist.
- **ALLOWED_WITH_CONDITIONS** — allowed only when explicit category controls are satisfied.
- **UNKNOWN_HOLD** — unclear or high-risk category fails closed pending legal/compliance review.

No high-risk category defaults to public merely because no rule has been configured.

## 3. Core marketplace legal baseline

Republic Act No. 11967, the Internet Transactions Act of 2023, applies to covered B2B/B2C internet transactions involving the Philippine market.

Section 15 provides for takedown of listings/offers involving prohibited or regulated goods/services and expressly gives examples including endangered animals, illicit drugs, fireworks and other explosives, and counterfeit goods. It also covers certain online activities threatening public/personal safety or compromising financial/personal information.

For e-marketplaces, Section 21 establishes diligence requirements including, as far as practicable before listing, online Merchant identity or business-registration information, geographic address, contact details and, where the service is connected to a regulated profession, relevant professional-body details.

Business & Life therefore treats pre-publication identity/category controls as a consumer-protection control, not merely an optional moderation feature.

Primary source:
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html

DTI overview:
https://ecommerce.dti.gov.ph/internet-transactions-act-of-2023/

## 4. PROHIBITED during the PH pilot

### 4.1 Illicit dangerous drugs and unlawful precursor trade

PROHIBITED:
- illicit dangerous drugs;
- unlawful controlled precursors or essential chemicals;
- unlawful brokerage, delivery, distribution or transport of such substances;
- drug paraphernalia where the offer is intended for unlawful dangerous-drug use.

Basis:
Republic Act No. 9165.
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2002/ra_9165_2002.html

### 4.2 Firearms, ammunition, explosives and incendiary devices

Business & Life adopts a stricter **platform policy** for the controlled pilot: firearms, ammunition, explosive devices, incendiary devices and their controlled trade are not supported.

This policy is intentionally broader than a statement that every such item is unlawful in every circumstance.

Relevant legal sources:
- Republic Act No. 10591:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10591_2013.html
- Republic Act No. 9516:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2008/ra_9516_2008.html

### 4.3 Firecrackers and pyrotechnics

PILOT_HOLD / PROHIBITED from marketplace publication during the controlled pilot.

Republic Act No. 7183 regulates manufacture, sale and distribution, prohibits certain devices and requires licensing/permits for lawful regulated activity. Business & Life will not build that licensing workflow during the initial pilot.

Source:
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1992/ra_7183_1992.html

### 4.4 Counterfeit, pirated or clearly infringing goods

PROHIBITED:
- counterfeit goods;
- unauthorized trademark copies offered as genuine;
- pirated goods/content offered through the commerce surface;
- repeat re-listing of content previously removed for verified IP infringement.

Sources:
- Republic Act No. 8293:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8293_1997.html
- IPOPHL Intellectual Property Rights Enforcement Office:
  https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/intellectual-property-rights-enforcement-office-ieo/

A complaint alone is not automatically proof of infringement. Business & Life must preserve evidence, verify the complaint where appropriate and maintain an appeal/review path.

### 4.5 Stolen goods and fencing indicators

PROHIBITED:
- goods known to be stolen;
- offers where verified evidence establishes that the seller is dealing in property derived from theft/robbery;
- deliberate concealment of provenance for high-risk goods after review.

Relevant Philippine law includes Presidential Decree No. 1612 (Anti-Fencing Law).

Business & Life should require provenance evidence for selected high-risk second-hand categories rather than treating all used goods as suspicious.

### 4.6 Unlawful wildlife trade

PROHIBITED:
- illegal trade in protected/endangered wildlife;
- unlawful specimens, parts or derivatives;
- listings that lack required lawful-authority evidence for a restricted wildlife transaction.

Business & Life pilot policy should fail closed on live wildlife and wildlife-derived products unless a later legal/compliance pack explicitly enables a lawful category.

Basis:
Republic Act No. 9147.
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2001/ra_9147_2001.html

### 4.7 Human trafficking, forced labor and exploitation

PROHIBITED:
- recruitment/transport/harboring/offers for exploitation;
- forced labor, slavery, involuntary servitude or debt-bondage arrangements;
- trafficking-related sexual exploitation;
- sale/removal of human organs;
- child exploitation or child-trading offers.

Basis:
Republic Act No. 11862.
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11862_2022.html

### 4.8 OSAEC / CSAEM

ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED:
- child sexual abuse or exploitation material;
- streaming/live-streaming or facilitation of online sexual abuse or exploitation of children;
- any commerce/service offer whose purpose is child sexual exploitation.

Republic Act No. 11930 imposes special duties on internet intermediaries, including terms prohibitions, preservation, takedown/removal and reporting duties in covered circumstances. These cases require a dedicated high-priority procedure and must never be handled as an ordinary catalogue dispute.

Source:
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11930_2022.html

### 4.9 Cybercrime services, malware and stolen credentials

PROHIBITED when intended for unlawful use:
- hacking-for-hire;
- phishing kits/services;
- credential/password/access-code theft or sale;
- malware/virus deployment services;
- tools primarily offered to commit illegal access, interception, data/system interference or computer fraud;
- identity-theft services.

Legitimate defensive security, authorized testing and ordinary IT repair must not be confused with criminal services.

Basis:
Republic Act No. 10175.
https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10175_2012.html

### 4.10 Forged identity, licence or credential services

PROHIBITED:
- forged government IDs;
- forged permits/licences/certificates;
- falsified professional credentials;
- services whose advertised purpose is impersonation, fraud or bypassing a lawful verification process.

## 5. RESTRICTED_REVIEW / PILOT_HOLD

### 5.1 Medicines and regulated health products

RESTRICTED_REVIEW.

Do not allow a health-product listing merely because the Merchant types a product name.

Required future controls include, as applicable:
- verified FDA-authorized establishment status;
- verified product registration/authorization;
- permitted online-sale/ordering activity;
- prescription/non-prescription distinction;
- advertising restrictions;
- storage/distribution requirements.

Sources:
- FDA Advisory 2019-154:
  https://www.fda.gov.ph/fda-advisory-no-2019-154-internet-sales-and-access-to-safe-medicine/
- FDA Advisory 2022-0108:
  https://www.fda.gov.ph/fda-advisory-no-2022-0108-reiteration-of-warning-against-the-promotion-and-advertisement-of-prescription-drug-products/

Until the exact production workflow is legally reviewed, prescription medicines remain PILOT_HOLD.

### 5.2 Vaporized nicotine / novel tobacco products

PILOT_HOLD for the initial Business & Life launch.

Republic Act No. 11900 includes minimum-age/proof-of-age rules and specific online-trade requirements. Business & Life should not enable this category until seller-registration, product-compliance, age verification and delivery-age verification are implemented and reviewed.

Source:
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11900_2022.html

### 5.3 Tobacco products

PILOT_HOLD until the applicable retail/age-verification/advertising rule set is fully sourced and implemented.

### 5.4 Alcoholic beverages

UNKNOWN_HOLD / PILOT_HOLD until a current Philippine legal source pack and reliable age-controlled ordering/delivery flow are approved.

### 5.5 Regulated professional services

RESTRICTED_REVIEW where Philippine law requires a professional licence, registration or supervised practice.

The rule must be category-specific rather than assuming every local trade is regulated in the same way.

Example:
Republic Act No. 7920 regulates the practice of electrical engineering/electrical work and prohibits specified practice without the required registration/licence.
https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1995/ra_7920_1995.html

Business & Life must map each regulated Local Services category to its own verified requirement set.

### 5.6 Financial, investment, loan and exchange services

UNKNOWN_HOLD / PILOT_HOLD until the competent regulator, licence model and Business & Life role are verified.

Do not allow a generic Local Services category to become an unreviewed route for:
- investment solicitation;
- lending;
- remittance;
- money exchange;
- crypto exchange/custody;
- securities or regulated financial advice.

### 5.7 Hazardous chemicals / regulated pesticides / controlled precursors

UNKNOWN_HOLD pending a category-specific Philippine source pack.

## 6. Enforcement model

### 6.1 Before publication

For Merchant, Supplier and Local Services publication:
1. resolve country = PH;
2. resolve category;
3. evaluate active versioned rule;
4. if PROHIBITED -> reject publication;
5. if RESTRICTED_REVIEW -> quarantine/unpublished until evidence passes;
6. if PILOT_HOLD or UNKNOWN_HOLD -> remain unpublished;
7. if ALLOWED_WITH_CONDITIONS -> evaluate conditions;
8. store the rule/version used for the decision.

### 6.2 Detection signals are not guilt findings

Keyword, image, pricing, behaviour and complaint signals may trigger review, but a machine-generated signal must not label a person a criminal.

Possible actions:
- allow;
- request information;
- quarantine listing;
- temporarily restrict publication;
- remove verified prohibited content;
- escalate to Trust & Safety;
- preserve evidence;
- suspend a capability/profile where proportionate and authorized.

### 6.3 Reposting

A previously removed prohibited listing must not be trivially republished with a new title.

Use:
- canonical category;
- seller/entity linkage;
- content/media hashes where lawful and appropriate;
- product identifiers;
- prior enforcement references.

Do not use invasive identity-linking beyond the documented Trust & Safety purpose.

### 6.4 Authority requests

Takedown/compliance requests must record:
- issuing authority;
- request/order reference;
- date/time received;
- exact listing/profile/content scope;
- legal/process basis;
- evidence preserved;
- action taken;
- actor;
- review/expiry state.

Do not delete historical accounting/payment evidence merely because public content is removed.

## 7. Consumer reporting

Public product/service/profile surfaces should provide a structured Report action.

Suggested reasons:
- illegal/restricted item;
- counterfeit/IP concern;
- stolen goods concern;
- fraud/scam;
- dangerous/unsafe product;
- false licence/credential;
- harassment/exploitation;
- privacy/data misuse;
- other.

Reports are allegations until reviewed.

## 9. Appeals and fairness

Where appropriate:
- tell the affected user which policy category caused the action;
- do not expose detection internals that would materially enable evasion;
- allow additional evidence;
- allow authorized human review;
- record the result;
- reinstate content where the restriction was erroneous.

Emergency/legally mandated removals may require immediate action first, with subsequent process as permitted by law.

## 10. Launch gate

The PH public pilot is not READY until:
- the initial category taxonomy is encoded;
- prohibited categories fail closed before publication;
- restricted categories cannot self-approve;
- Merchant identity/business evidence required by the marketplace model is implemented;
- Report flow and Trust & Safety queue exist;
- authority takedown workflow exists;
- OSAEC/CSAEM escalation procedure exists;
- policy decisions and appeals are auditable.

## 11. Review cadence

Review:
- before EFFECTIVE publication;
- when a relevant Philippine law/regulation changes;
- when DTI/FDA/PNP/IPOPHL/other competent authority guidance changes;
- after a material incident reveals a taxonomy gap;
- before enabling a currently held category.

No PH rule is automatically copied to another country edition.

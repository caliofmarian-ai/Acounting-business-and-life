# Curriculum — Merchant

## Audience

Approved Merchant profile holders and authorized business operators.

## Learning objectives

- Use correct business workspace.
- Process orders/payment/presence accurately.
- Record business money truthfully.
- Manage stock/products/procurement.
- Understand compliance responsibility.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Business workspace
- business tenancy
- Cash/GCash/Bank/Other
- private ledger boundary

### Module 2 — Orders
- payment vs fulfilment
- presence gate
- remote cash trust
- credit/receivable

### Module 3 — Stock/products
- inventory
- recipes
- stock consumption/reversal
- profitability estimates

### Module 4 — Procurement
- accepted Supplier
- PO snapshots
- physical receiving
- real payment

### Module 5 — Cash/reconciliation
- opening/closing
- variance
- linked financial events
- audit correction

### Module 6 — Compliance/privacy
- business registration/LGU/BIR responsibilities
- Customer data
- incident/support

## Practical exercises

1. Process a new Customer cash pickup requiring presence.
2. Close a sample day with a variance and choose the correct response.
3. Receive a partial Supplier order and explain the accounting/stock consequences.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Can distinguish order total from paid/outstanding.
- Chooses correct money account.
- Does not duplicate linked payment transactions.
- Knows platform approval is not business permit.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Marking unpaid money paid.
- Starting gated order improperly.
- Fabricating cash count.
- Accessing another business ledger.
- Recording goods/payment before real event.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

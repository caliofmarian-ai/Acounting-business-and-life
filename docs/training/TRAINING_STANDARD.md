# Business & Life — Training and Competency Standard

## Purpose

Define how Business & Life decides that a role/profile holder has received the information and practical preparation necessary to use assigned authority responsibly.

## Training types

- Orientation — understand product/business, role and core rules.
- Workflow training — perform specific SOPs.
- Compliance training — understand requirement/evidence/legal boundaries.
- Privileged-access training — Admin/support/private-data controls.
- Money-handling training — payment/accounting/cash/reconciliation.
- Safety training — Delivery/Service/incident response where relevant.
- Refresher — repeat/update after time or material change.
- Remedial training — required after a material error/incident/control breach.

## Competency status

Recommended:
- NOT_STARTED
- IN_PROGRESS
- COMPLETED_TRAINING
- COMPETENCY_CHECK_PENDING
- COMPETENT
- REFRESHER_DUE
- RETRAINING_REQUIRED
- SUSPENDED_PENDING_REVIEW

"Completed training" and "Competent" are intentionally separate.

## Evidence

Each controlled training completion should record:
- participant/account;
- role/profile;
- curriculum ID/version;
- modules;
- start/completion;
- trainer/system;
- assessment outcome;
- practical exercise outcome;
- acknowledgement;
- next review/refresher if approved;
- related external credential references if separately verified.

## Assessment principle

Do not grant competency merely because someone opened/read a document when the role controls money, private evidence, safety or privileged access.

High-risk functions should use:
- scenario questions;
- practical exercise;
- error recognition;
- escalation decision.

## Retraining triggers

Examples:
- material MAJOR SOP change;
- material legal/compliance change;
- repeated process errors;
- unauthorized data access;
- improper payment/accounting action;
- false verification/approval;
- serious safety incident;
- failed audit;
- return after prolonged inactivity where required.

## External qualifications

PRC/TESDA/LTO/other credentials remain separate from Business & Life training records.

The platform can map external evidence into the training/compliance profile but must not generate or imitate the external credential.

## Access governance

Training may become a prerequisite for role authorization after the corresponding RBAC/authorization layer supports:
- curriculum version;
- competency status;
- expiry/refresher;
- exception approval;
- audit.

Until then, these curricula are controlled operating policy/design, not a technical access control.

# Curriculum — Local Services Provider

## Audience

Approved Service Provider profiles.

## Learning objectives

- Maintain truthful public/private profile data.
- Quote and perform work through correct lifecycle.
- Understand credential/category boundaries.
- Protect Customer data/portfolio consent.
- Use verified review flow correctly.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Profile/visibility
- public vs relationship-only vs private
- public reputation opt-in
- private CV/evidence

### Module 2 — Services/credentials
- category scope
- submitted/verified/rejected/expired
- PRC/TESDA not universal

### Module 3 — Request/quote
- clear scope
- quote amount/note
- Customer acceptance

### Module 4 — Job lifecycle
- scheduled/in-progress/completed
- Customer confirmation
- dispute

### Module 5 — Portfolio/reviews
- Customer publication consent
- verified review after confirmed completion

### Module 6 — Privacy/safety
- address/contact use
- property care
- incident escalation

## Practical exercises

1. Respond to a sample request with a transparent quote.
2. Choose correct action when requested work is outside authorized scope.
3. Decide if a job-linked photo may appear publicly.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Understands completion vs Customer confirmation.
- Understands credential status.
- Uses Customer data only for service purpose.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Falsifying credentials/reviews.
- Performing prohibited/unauthorized scope.
- Publishing Customer content without consent.
- Marking incomplete work completed.

## Required controlled documents

- PLAYBOOK_SERVICE_PROVIDER.md
- SOP_LOCAL_SERVICES.md
- SERVICE_PROVIDER_AGREEMENT.md
- DELIVERY_LOCAL_SERVICES_RESEARCH.md

## Training evidence generated

- Training acknowledgement.
- Job-lifecycle scenario.
- Privacy/credential check.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

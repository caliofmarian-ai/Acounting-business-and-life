# Curriculum — Business Cash / Finance Operator

## Audience

Authorized Merchant/Supplier staff handling cash, payments, accounting entries and reconciliation.

## Learning objectives

- Record real money movement in correct account.
- Reconcile Cash.
- Recognize linked financial entries.
- Handle corrections with audit.
- Escalate discrepancies/fraud.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Money accounts
- Cash
- GCash
- Bank
- Other
- business workspace

### Module 2 — Transactions
- sale
- business expense
- money received
- personal withdrawal
- adjustment

### Module 3 — Linked events
- order payment
- Supplier payment/receipt
- remittance
- product sale
- no duplicate entry

### Module 4 — Reconciliation
- opening cash
- expected
- actual
- variance
- investigation

### Module 5 — Receivables/payables
- paid/outstanding
- Customer credit
- Supplier obligations

### Module 6 — Corrections/audit
- eligible manual corrections
- protected sources
- before/after/reason

## Practical exercises

1. Classify ten money movements into correct type/account.
2. Reconcile a cash-day discrepancy.
3. Identify a linked transaction that must not be manually corrected.

## Competency / knowledge check

- No confusion between sale and actual payment.
- Correct account selection.
- Explains outstanding receivable/payable.
- Preserves audit history.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Fabricated payment/cash count.
- Duplicate linked transaction.
- Editing another business ledger.
- Using adjustment to hide discrepancy.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

# Curriculum — Supplier

## Audience

Approved Supplier profile/business operators.

## Learning objectives

- Maintain accurate catalog.
- Use accepted Merchant relationships.
- Confirm/fulfil POs accurately.
- Understand partial receiving/payment.
- Use Supplier accounting without duplication.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Profile/catalog
- pack/base-unit conversion
- price/minimum/lead time
- availability

### Module 2 — Trusted relationships
- invite/accept/revoke
- Merchant-specific relationship

### Module 3 — PO response
- snapshot vs confirmed values
- partial acceptance
- ETA/status

### Module 4 — Fulfilment/receiving
- delivered does not equal Merchant-received
- partial receiving

### Module 5 — Payment/accounting
- partial/paid
- linked Supplier receipt
- no duplicate manual entry

### Module 6 — Compliance/privacy
- business/product obligations
- Merchant data confidentiality

## Practical exercises

1. Respond to PO with reduced confirmed quantity.
2. Explain why later catalog price does not rewrite PO snapshot.
3. Review a partial payment and Supplier receivable.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Understands relationship prerequisite.
- Distinguishes confirmed and received quantity.
- Does not report false fulfilment/payment.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Falsifying availability/delivery.
- Changing historical order facts.
- Duplicating linked accounting.
- Misusing Merchant data.

## Required controlled documents

- PLAYBOOK_SUPPLIER.md
- SOP_SUPPLIER_PROCUREMENT.md
- Supplier agreement framework
- AUTHORIZATION_MATRIX.md

## Training evidence generated

- Training acknowledgement.
- PO/receiving/payment competency result.

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

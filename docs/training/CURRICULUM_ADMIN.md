# Curriculum — Privileged Admin / Territory Operations

## Audience

Super Admin, Country Admin and Territory Admin according to assigned scope.

## Learning objectives

- Use privileged access only within delegated scope.
- Distinguish platform authority from government authority.
- Make approval/suspension decisions with reason/evidence.
- Protect private data/evidence.
- Recognize when to escalate legal, finance, security and safety issues.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- Assigned controlled Admin role.
- Acknowledged Code of Conduct and privileged Admin agreement.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Authority and RBAC
- role/territory/country scope
- least privilege
- separation of duties
- auditability

### Module 2 — Profile governance
- application states
- evidence
- authorization/suspension/expiry
- platform vs government approval

### Module 3 — Privacy and evidence
- purpose limitation
- incident/credential confidentiality
- no curiosity access
- data minimization

### Module 4 — Money boundaries
- never fabricate payment/settlement
- finance escalation
- business ledgers are scoped

### Module 5 — Incidents and escalation
- support vs incident
- serious-risk escalation
- authority/legal request routing

## Practical exercises

1. Review a fictional Merchant application and identify missing evidence without inventing a legal requirement.
2. Handle a conflict-of-interest scenario and choose recusal/escalation.
3. Classify a payment dispute that Admin cannot resolve by changing status.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Can explain delegated authority limits.
- Can identify private evidence that should not be public.
- Can distinguish platform authorization from an LGU/BIR/PRC/LTO authorization.
- Can select correct escalation route.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Browsing private records without business need.
- Approving own related party without conflict disclosure.
- Changing payment/accounting facts without evidence.
- Representing platform status as government approval.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

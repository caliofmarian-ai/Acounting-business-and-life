# Curriculum — Compliance / Credential Reviewer

## Audience

Reviewers of profile/business/credential evidence.

## Learning objectives

- Verify sources before requirements.
- Separate source verification from applicability.
- Review evidence consistently.
- Protect private evidence.
- Avoid false government/platform equivalence.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Source hierarchy
- official primary sources
- law vs regulation vs guidance vs platform policy
- jurisdiction

### Module 2 — Applicability
- entity/activity/territory/vehicle/category
- RESEARCH_REQUIRED states
- LGU-specific rules

### Module 3 — Evidence review
- issuer/reference/dates/expiry
- authenticity red flags
- verification status/reason

### Module 4 — Professional/vehicle categories
- PRC/TESDA category specificity
- LTO evidence
- LTFRB applicability is model-specific

### Module 5 — Change control
- source registry
- review dates
- dependent SOP/forms/training updates

## Practical exercises

1. Classify a DTI BN certificate without calling it full authority to operate.
2. Decide whether PRC should be required for an unmapped trade.
3. Handle an LTFRB question where applicability is not established.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Uses SOURCE_VERIFIED/APPLICABILITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED/RESEARCH_REQUIRED correctly.
- Records source ID and reason.
- Does not invent legal requirements.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Approving fabricated/irrelevant evidence.
- Treating one LGU rule as national.
- Requiring regulator credential without applicability basis.
- Publishing private evidence.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

# Curriculum — Support Agent

## Audience

Support Agents and operators handling user requests.

## Learning objectives

- Resolve simple issues through self-service guidance.
- Protect account/private data.
- Never collect passwords/secrets.
- Escalate money, approval, safety and legal issues correctly.
- Keep useful case notes.

## Prerequisite knowledge / access

- None beyond applicable onboarding prerequisites.

## Modules

### Module 1 — Product navigation
- five profiles
- Help Center
- common order/delivery/service states

### Module 2 — Identity/security
- account recovery
- privacy-safe identity verification
- no-password rule

### Module 3 — Domain triage
- order
- accounting
- Supplier
- Delivery
- Local Services
- profile approval

### Module 4 — High-risk escalation
- money
- fraud/safety
- privacy/security
- legal/compliance

### Module 5 — Case records
- minimal data
- clear notes
- handoff owner
- resolution/closure

## Practical exercises

1. Route ten sample cases to self-service/support/finance/admin/incident/security.
2. Respond to a user offering a password and safely stop collection.
3. Explain why Support cannot mark an unpaid order as paid.

## Competency / knowledge check

- Correct escalation classification.
- No-password/no-unnecessary-PII behavior.
- Uses public Help Center before collecting more private data when possible.

## Critical errors / retraining triggers

- Requesting password/token.
- Sharing another user's data.
- Promising refund/approval without authority.
- Editing money/status to silence a complaint.

## Required controlled documents

- JD_SUPPORT_AGENT.md
- SOP_SUPPORT_TRIAGE.md
- SOP_INCIDENTS.md
- Privacy Notice framework
- Code of Conduct

## Training evidence generated

- Training acknowledgement.
- Triage assessment.
- Observed-case review where available.

## Access dependency

Training is required by policy but runtime access dependency is not enforced until the corresponding authorization/RBAC workflow implements it.

## Refresher

Refresher cadence is **TO BE APPROVED**. A refresher is also required after material MAJOR changes to the role's SOP, legal/compliance requirements, money-handling controls or privileged access.

## Certification boundary

A Business & Life training-completion record is an internal/platform record. It must not be represented as a PRC licence, TESDA National Certificate, government certificate or other external professional qualification unless that external credential was separately issued and verified.

# Business & Life — Controlled Philippines Pilot Proposal

## Objective

Prove one repeatable local economic loop in one small territory before broader expansion.

## Proposed initial scope

- Customer account onboarding;
- one or a small number of approved local Merchants;
- Food/Non-food ordering as applicable;
- cash pickup rules and configured online payment only when production-ready;
- Merchant accounting/stock;
- optional approved Courier;
- support/incident path;
- Admin approval/suspension capability;
- documented SOPs/compliance.

Supplier and Local Services should activate only when their own launch gates are ready.

## Pilot success is operational, not just acquisition

The pilot should demonstrate:
- Customers can complete legitimate orders;
- Merchant can see/prepare/complete them;
- money received and outstanding remain correct;
- stock/accounting remain explainable;
- delivery can complete securely where used;
- issues can be handled;
- operator roles can follow documentation;
- legal/compliance blockers are identified and tracked.

## Gate framework

Every required launch item is:
- READY — evidence exists;
- HOLD — blocker known;
- UNKNOWN — evidence missing.

UNKNOWN is never interpreted as READY.

## Pilot evidence pack

- territory definition;
- Merchant/business compliance evidence;
- participant authorization;
- test/CI/deployment evidence;
- payment rules/provider evidence;
- SOP acknowledgements;
- privacy/security baseline;
- backup/recovery readiness;
- support/incident readiness;
- authority/partner communications;
- KPI baseline.

## Pilot review

At defined checkpoints ask:
- What completed successfully?
- Where did money/stock/status diverge?
- Which user/operator step was confusing?
- Which requirement was not documented?
- Which incidents occurred?
- What must be fixed before expansion?

## Expansion decision

Move to the next territory only when the existing territory can be operated from documented procedures and measured evidence rather than founder memory.

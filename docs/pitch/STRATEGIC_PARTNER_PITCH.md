# Business & Life — Strategic Partner Pitch

## Purpose

Provide a reusable source for discussions with payment, logistics, insurance, training/certification, accounting, technology and institutional partners.

## Partnership principle

A partnership should strengthen a defined economic workflow rather than add a logo or disconnected feature.

## What Business & Life provides

- multi-profile local ecosystem;
- Merchant/Supplier economic workspaces;
- structured Customer/order/procurement/service flows;
- Delivery workflow;
- controlled role/compliance documentation;
- country/territory architecture;
- integration opportunities.

## What we may seek

### Payment partner
- server-confirmed payment evidence;
- Philippines-supported methods;
- reconciliation/webhooks;
- settlement/refund support;
- clear processor fees.

### Logistics / mobility partner
- capacity;
- vehicle/driver/insurance requirements;
- routing/operations;
- safety;
- settlement evidence.

### Insurance partner
- product fit for Merchant/Courier/Service Provider risks;
- eligibility/evidence;
- claims workflow;
- privacy-safe data exchange.

### Training/certification partner
- recognized/relevant training;
- competency mapping;
- verification;
- renewals;
- participant development.

### Accounting/bookkeeping partner
- record review;
- business onboarding;
- tax/compliance handoff;
- evidence interpretation;
- no false automated filing claims.

### Technology partner
- clear data-processing purpose;
- security;
- API/integration boundary;
- auditability;
- country support.

## Due diligence before commitment

- legal entity/authority;
- service coverage;
- licensing/regulatory status where applicable;
- pricing/commercial terms;
- data/privacy/security;
- operational SLAs;
- complaints/incidents;
- termination/continuity;
- reconciliation/financial controls if money is involved.

## Partnership claim rule

Do not use partner name/logo publicly as an active Business & Life partner until the relationship and permitted public representation are confirmed.

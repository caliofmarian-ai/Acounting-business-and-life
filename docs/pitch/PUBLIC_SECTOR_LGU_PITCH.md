# Business & Life Philippines — LGU / Public-Sector Pitch

## Opening

Business & Life is developing a mobile-first local economic platform intended to help local Customers, small businesses, Suppliers, Delivery Providers and skilled Service Providers participate through clearer, more traceable workflows.

We are seeking a **controlled, cooperative pilot relationship**, not automatic endorsement.

## Local problem we aim to help organize

Small businesses and informal/local economic activity may rely on disconnected tools and paper/manual processes. Business & Life is designed to organize:
- Customer orders;
- Merchant accounting/cash/stock;
- Supplier procurement;
- delivery handoff;
- local service requests;
- profile/evidence controls;
- private incident handling.

## Pilot approach

We intend to start small:
- one clearly defined territory;
- limited invited Merchants/operational participants;
- documented participant requirements;
- support and incident path;
- measurable pilot acceptance gates;
- no broad geographic rollout until reliability is demonstrated.

## Role of the LGU / authority

We may request:
- confirmation of local permit/registration process;
- identification of the competent local office for specific activities;
- clarification of territory-specific requirements;
- opportunity to present the pilot model;
- feedback on local economic/community priorities;
- coordination where appropriate.

We do **not** ask the authority to treat an internal Business & Life approval as a government permit.

## Compliance posture

Business & Life maintains an official-source registry and separates:
- national registration/tax authorities;
- local LGU requirements;
- profession/category credentials;
- vehicle/transport requirements;
- platform policy.

Exact local requirements are intended to be researched with the relevant LGU rather than guessed from another city.

## Privacy and trust posture

The architecture separates:
- public profile/store information;
- private business financial records;
- private CV/credential evidence;
- private incidents/evidence;
- purpose-limited Courier location.

## Documents available for an authority discussion

- product/pilot brief;
- role/responsibility map;
- Philippines compliance source summary;
- authorization matrix;
- privacy baseline;
- authority questions/request list;
- document submission index;
- meeting minutes/follow-up record.

## Proposed pilot discussion questions

1. Which local business-permit/BPLO/BOSS/eBOSS path applies to the intended pilot business participants?
2. Are there local clearances/inspections specific to intended Merchant activities?
3. Which local office should Business & Life consult for delivery operations?
4. Are there local livelihood/MSME/digitalization initiatives relevant to the pilot?
5. Which official local ordinances/process documents should be included in the territory compliance pack?
6. What reporting/coordination would the LGU expect from a small private pilot, if any?

## Closing

Our objective is to build the local pilot with documented responsibilities and verified requirements rather than assume compliance. Any cooperation, approval or guidance will be represented only according to the actual written/official basis.

# Join Business & Life Philippines

## Merchant

Business & Life is designed to help a Merchant connect storefront/order operations with:
- cash and money accounts;
- inventory;
- products/recipes;
- receivables;
- Suppliers;
- delivery;
- reports.

Participation is governed and does not replace business registration/tax/LGU obligations.

## Supplier

Suppliers can:
- maintain a catalog;
- accept Merchant relationships;
- receive structured purchase orders;
- confirm quantities/prices;
- manage fulfilment;
- use business accounting.

The relationship remains explicit: a Merchant cannot silently declare an unknown account a trusted Supplier.

## Delivery Provider

The Delivery profile is designed around:
- eligibility/evidence;
- availability;
- assignment;
- active route/location;
- secure Customer handoff.

Approval comes before Availability. Platform eligibility does not replace government/vehicle requirements that apply to the chosen service/vehicle model.

## Local Service Provider

Service Providers can:
- build a professional profile;
- select services;
- submit credentials/portfolio;
- receive requests;
- quote;
- complete jobs;
- earn verified in-app reviews after Customer confirmation.

Credential requirements depend on the actual service category; not every trade is treated as PRC-regulated.

## What Business & Life expects from participants

- truthful identity/business/profile information;
- honest pricing/catalog/status information;
- accurate money records where applicable;
- privacy/confidentiality;
- current required evidence;
- respectful Customer/business conduct;
- use of Incident/support when something goes wrong;
- no false claims of licence/approval.

## What Business & Life should provide

- clear role playbook;
- applicable SOPs;
- public Help Center;
- requirement/checklist information;
- status visibility;
- controlled forms/documents;
- support/escalation;
- transparent commercial terms when fees are activated.

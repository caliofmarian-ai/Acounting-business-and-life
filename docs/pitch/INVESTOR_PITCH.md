# Business & Life — Investor Pitch Source

## Investment thesis

Business & Life is building a local economic operating layer rather than a single-purpose marketplace.

The thesis is that local commerce becomes more useful and defensible when the platform connects:
- demand;
- Merchant operations;
- accounting;
- procurement;
- delivery;
- local skilled work;
- trust/compliance;
- documentation.

## Why this architecture can compound

A successful transaction can strengthen multiple product layers:
- Customer order creates demand evidence;
- Merchant records improve business visibility;
- stock creates procurement need;
- Supplier workflow creates B2B network value;
- Delivery completes fulfilment;
- support/incident and verified reviews improve trust;
- documentation/compliance reduces operational ambiguity.

## Go-to-market discipline

The strategy is not "launch everywhere."

It is:
1. prove one Philippines local economic cell;
2. document the operating model;
3. establish reliable unit/operational evidence;
4. replicate to next territory;
5. later create isolated country editions.

## Current evidence

Current evidence should be demonstrated with:
- live product/runtime;
- repository/test history;
- public Help Center;
- controlled business documentation;
- pilot acceptance evidence once available.

No traction/GMV/revenue numbers should be presented until measured and auditable.

## Monetization architecture — not yet rate-approved

Potential families:
- marketplace/service fees;
- delivery allocations;
- Local Services fee;
- operator/country/territory participation;
- premium business tooling;
- compliance/document services where lawful;
- Platform Store;
- commercial partnerships.

Investment materials must distinguish "supported by architecture" from "activated and producing revenue."

## Risks we are explicitly managing

- scope expansion before pilot proof;
- financial-record inconsistency;
- cross-business data leakage;
- regulatory misclassification;
- improper role activation;
- privacy/evidence misuse;
- premature monetization;
- growth before operational quality.

## Use-of-funds framework

A future financing plan may allocate capital to:
- product/engineering/security;
- payment/reconciliation;
- compliance/legal;
- pilot operations;
- local onboarding/support;
- partnerships;
- infrastructure/data;
- measured growth.

Exact amounts and runway are owner/finance decisions and must be added only from an approved financial model.

## Investor evidence checklist before external use

- current capitalization/company entity facts;
- audited/verified founder/company information;
- product demo;
- measured pilot metrics;
- real financial model;
- legal/compliance risk review;
- data room;
- IP/brand status;
- team/role plan;
- financing terms.

Until populated, this document is a pitch **source**, not a final fundraising representation.

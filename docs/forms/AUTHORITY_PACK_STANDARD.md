# Local Authority Relationship Pack Standard

## Objective

Ensure every formal interaction with an LGU/local authority is organized, factual, traceable and consistent.

## Pack contents

### 1. Authority identity
- authority/LGU name;
- office/department;
- contact person/role;
- official contact channels;
- jurisdiction;
- source/website.

### 2. Business & Life introduction
- company/platform identity;
- Philippines Edition description;
- purpose of contact;
- pilot territory;
- profiles/services in scope;
- data/privacy/compliance posture.

### 3. Request / question list
Examples:
- confirmation of local permit requirements;
- pilot presentation request;
- clarification of local BPLO/BOSS process;
- referral to competent authority;
- verification of a local requirement/source.

### 4. Supporting documents
Select only relevant documents:
- business/entity registration evidence;
- BIR evidence;
- project/pilot brief;
- privacy/security summary;
- participant/role model;
- compliance checklist;
- diagrams;
- official forms required by authority.

### 5. Submission index
Every item:
- index number;
- title;
- version/date;
- issuer;
- confidentiality;
- pages/files;
- official vs Business & Life generated.

### 6. Meeting brief
- objectives;
- attendees;
- known facts;
- open questions;
- commitments we may make;
- commitments requiring Project Owner approval;
- prohibited claims.

### 7. Meeting minutes
Record:
- date/time/location;
- participants;
- factual guidance received;
- documents requested;
- responsible person;
- deadlines;
- next action;
- source/reference if authority cites rule/ordinance.

### 8. Follow-up
- written recap;
- missing documents;
- clarification request;
- status;
- next meeting/submission.

## Communication rules

Never say:
- "the LGU approved us" without documentary basis;
- "this permit is not required" unless verified by competent authority/source;
- "we comply with all laws" as a blanket claim;
- "this app replaces government registration";
- "our internal certificate is an official permit."

## Records

Authority communications should create a controlled contact log with:
- actor;
- authority;
- purpose;
- date;
- documents shared;
- commitments;
- outcome;
- next review.

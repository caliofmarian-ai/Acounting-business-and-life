# SOP — Legal / Compliance Source Verification and Requirement Version Control

## Purpose

Prevent stale, unsupported or jurisdiction-mismatched legal guidance from becoming a Business & Life rule or application gate.

## Scope

Applies to legislation, regulation, authority guidance, licence/permit checklists and compliance requirements.

## Roles

- Researcher locates official source.
- Compliance Reviewer verifies content/applicability.
- Country Admin approves operational interpretation.
- Documentation Governance controls versions/dependencies.

## Prerequisites

- Specific question/profile/activity/territory identified.
- Source registry available.

## Inputs / evidence

- Official authority source.
- Law/regulation/guidance version/date.
- Jurisdiction/activity.
- Existing checklist/SOP/form dependencies.

## Procedure

1. State the exact compliance question before researching.
2. Identify competent authority and prefer primary official source.
3. Record source ID, URL, authority, legal classification, date/effective information and verification date.
4. Separate source verification from applicability determination.
5. Map affected profile/activity/entity/vehicle/territory.
6. Define required evidence, expiry/renewal and reviewer only when supported.
7. Classify unresolved applicability as APPLICABILITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED or RESEARCH_REQUIRED rather than guessing.
8. Update authorization/checklist documents with source/version reference.
9. Identify dependent SOPs/forms/training/public Help content affected by the change.
10. Obtain required review/approval before marking a requirement effective.
11. Set review_due or monitoring responsibility.
12. When superseded, retain historical version and mark dependent content for review.

## Control points

- Official primary source preferred.
- Jurisdiction must be explicit.
- LAW/REGULATION/OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE/PLATFORM_POLICY/OPERATIONAL_INTERPRETATION/TEMPLATE_GUIDANCE remain distinct.
- No requirement becomes executable only because a secondary article says so.
- LGU/local rules remain territory-specific.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Copying another country's requirement.
- Treating one LGU checklist as national.
- Calling platform policy a law.
- Requiring PRC/TESDA/LTFRB without applicability evidence.
- Deleting superseded source history.

## Exceptions / escalation

- Official source unavailable/ambiguous → mark uncertainty and seek authority/qualified adviser.
- Conflicting sources → escalate; do not silently choose convenient interpretation.
- Emergency regulator change → temporary instruction with issuer/scope/expiry and formal review.

## Records / audit

- Source registry.
- Research note.
- Approval/review record.
- Changed requirement version.
- Dependency/change log.

## KPI / quality controls

- Requirements with official source coverage.
- Overdue legal reviews.
- Stale dependent documents.
- Incorrect applicability reversals.
- Time from verified change to controlled-document update.

## Version control

This SOP is a controlled DRAFT until approved/effective. If product behavior, legal/compliance requirements or delegated authority changes, this SOP must be reviewed before being treated as current.

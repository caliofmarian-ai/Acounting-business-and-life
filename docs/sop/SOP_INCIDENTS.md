# SOP — Private Incident Reporting, Evidence Handling and Escalation

## Purpose

Handle operational/safety/fraud/payment problems privately with purpose-limited evidence and an auditable timeline.

## Scope

Applies to current private incident system and future authorized triage roles.

## Roles

- Reporter submits own incident/evidence/follow-up.
- Support/Territory/Admin routes cases within authority.
- Trust & Safety investigates/escalates serious cases.

## Prerequisites

- Reporter authenticated.
- Problem appropriate for private incident rather than ordinary public review/help.

## Inputs / evidence

- Related type/record ID where applicable.
- Description.
- Images/PDF within limits.
- Follow-up note.
- Admin status/action/resolution where authorized.

## Procedure

1. Reporter selects the appropriate related type (order, delivery, Merchant, Courier, service job/provider, Supplier, payment or other).
2. Reporter describes what happened factually and submits only relevant evidence.
3. System enforces evidence limits: maximum 5 JPEG/PNG/WebP images up to 1.5 MB each; maximum 1 PDF up to 3 MB; total decoded evidence up to 10 MB.
4. Reporter reviews own incident in My reports and may add information.
5. Authorized triage reviews case based on urgency/risk and records actions.
6. Use supported status lifecycle: submitted, triaged, investigating, awaiting_information, escalated, resolved or dismissed.
7. Request additional information only when necessary.
8. Escalate safety, fraud, privacy, financial or legal concerns to the competent internal function.
9. When resolved/dismissed, record a resolution summary sufficient for audit without unnecessary sensitive detail.

## Control points

- Incident evidence is private, not public reputation.
- Access follows reporter/authorized Admin purpose.
- Evidence file limits are server-enforced.
- Admin actions/status changes create auditable case history.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Publishing private incident evidence as a public review.
- Collecting unrelated sensitive data.
- Copying evidence into informal channels without approved need.
- Closing serious case without reason/evidence.
- Retaliating against reporter for good-faith reporting.

## Exceptions / escalation

- Immediate physical danger → local emergency response first where appropriate, then platform incident.
- Data breach/security event → Security/Privacy incident procedure.
- Material payment fraud → Finance/Trust & Safety escalation.
- Legal/authority request → Country Admin/authorized legal process.

## Records / audit

- Incident report.
- Attachments.
- Private notes/timeline.
- Admin status/action.
- Resolution summary.
- Related operational record.

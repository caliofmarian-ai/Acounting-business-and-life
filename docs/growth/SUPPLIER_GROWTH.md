# Supplier Growth — V1

## Positioning
Business & Life Supplier is for eligible local producers, makers, wholesalers and supply businesses that work with trusted local Merchants.

**Supply local businesses. Keep orders, stock and payments easier to follow.**

## Current executable value
### Catalog
- catalog items;
- pack/base-unit conversion;
- price snapshots;
- availability and lead time.

### Trusted Merchant relationships
Supplier relationships are explicit and scoped. A Supplier is not automatically usable by every Merchant.

### Procurement
- purchase orders;
- amendments;
- ETA/readiness;
- pickup/delivery states;
- partial receiving.

### Supplier money
- business-scoped accounting workspace;
- procurement payments recorded only when money actually moves;
- historical evidence remains separate from later catalog changes.

## Target examples
- local food producer;
- ingredient producer/supplier;
- farmer/grower;
- packaging/material supplier;
- small manufacturer;
- wholesaler;
- family producer/business;
- other eligible local Supplier.

## Boundaries
Supplier is invite-only/Admin-approved in current governance.

Do not promise:
- guaranteed sales;
- guaranteed payment;
- automatic trust relationship with every Merchant;
- universal public marketplace discovery;
- automatic legal/tax compliance.

## Inspection protocol
After merge + verified deployment report PR/SHA, files/routes/screens, deployed SHA/status, direct inspection URL, exact in-app destination and remaining gates.

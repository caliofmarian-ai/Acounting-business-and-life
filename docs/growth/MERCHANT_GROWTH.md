# Merchant Growth — V1

## Positioning

Business & Life Merchant is for eligible local businesses such as restaurants, fast-food/takeaway businesses, bakeries, food stalls, sari-sari/convenience shops, small groceries and other local retail businesses.

The core message is:

**Run the business. Sell to customers. See where the money goes.**

## Current executable value

The campaign is grounded only in features already present in the application:

### Money
- Cash / GCash / Bank / Other balances where configured;
- sales and business expenses;
- daily reconciliation;
- receivables;
- audit corrections;
- reports.

### Stock and products
- inventory;
- reorder warnings;
- products/menu;
- recipes;
- ingredient consumption;
- unit cost and gross-margin visibility.

### Customers
- Marketplace storefront once approved/published;
- basket/checkout;
- customer orders;
- preparation/fulfilment states;
- order tracking.

### Local business network
- trusted Supplier relationships;
- procurement orders;
- Delivery workflow for eligible Business & Life customer orders;
- delivery fee kept separate from merchandise revenue.

## Important Merchant boundaries

Merchant is currently invite-only/Admin-approved.

A Merchant business/accounting workspace and a public Marketplace storefront are separate states.

Do not advertise:
- guaranteed profit;
- automatic Merchant approval;
- guaranteed tax/legal compliance;
- payment providers that are not configured;
- arbitrary external parcel delivery.

## Target merchant examples

- restaurant;
- fast-food / takeaway;
- bakery;
- food stall;
- sari-sari / convenience shop;
- small grocery;
- local retail shop;
- other eligible local Merchant.

## Inspection protocol

After merge + verified deployment report:
- PR and merge SHA;
- deployed SHA and Railway status;
- direct production inspection URL;
- route/files changed;
- exact in-app destination;
- gates that remain.

# Community Local Services — Growth V1

## Product promise

Business & Life Local Services has two public-facing purposes:

1. **Customer side:** find a local tradesperson/service provider and request work.
2. **Provider side:** promote your own skills/services and receive eligible work requests.

This Growth lane is grounded in the existing Local Services implementation from Issue #15 / PR #21.

## Beginner and experience-first positioning

A major acquisition message is that Business & Life should not be only for established firms or formally qualified professionals.

For **unrestricted services/tasks**, a person without a formal qualification may still build a Service Provider profile using:
- skills/services;
- structured work history;
- years of experience;
- CV/resume evidence;
- references where permitted;
- portfolio/work examples;
- availability and service area.

This can help a beginner build the first pieces of a visible work history.

### Transparency is mandatory

The platform must clearly distinguish:
- verified formal credential;
- uploaded but unverified credential;
- experience/CV only;
- no verified credential on file.

The campaign must never imply that a beginner is verified when they are not.

### Regulated/gated work remains gated

The beginner-friendly message is not permission to perform every type of work.

Credential-gated or regulated tasks remain unavailable until the configured requirements and approval process are satisfied.

## Customer acquisition message

### Main headline
**Need help? Find a local person who can do the job.**

Customer value:
- browse by task/category;
- service area;
- availability;
- experience;
- portfolio;
- indicative price model/range;
- credentials where available;
- verified completed-job/review evidence where enabled.

## Provider acquisition message

### Main headline
**Have a useful skill? Let local people find you.**

Provider value:
- one account;
- Service Provider profile;
- multiple services/tasks;
- experience and portfolio presentation;
- pricing/availability;
- local discovery;
- service requests;
- quote/job workflow;
- opportunity to build completed-job/review history.

## Beginner message

### Main headline
**You can start before you have a long work history.**

Approved supporting language:
- Start building your work history.
- Show your skills and portfolio.
- Receive service requests for eligible tasks.
- Experience matters too.
- Not every service requires a formal qualification.

Do not use:
- Anyone can do any job.
- Guaranteed work.
- Guaranteed income.
- No qualifications ever required.
- Verified professional without verification.

## Community angle

This is intentionally local-first.

Examples:
- someone needs furniture assembled;
- a wall painted;
- a garden cleaned;
- a phone repaired;
- a handyman for a small job;
- a carpenter;
- a cleaner;
- a tailor;
- a repair person.

The service should make it easier for local skills and local demand to find each other while preserving evidence, job history, quote/status workflow and safety/approval boundaries.

## Referral angle

Local Services participates in the account-level Growth referral system.

A provider may share:
- their referral link;
- QR;
- social creative;
- Story/Status;
- flyer/card;
- community/group message initiated by the user.

Referral attribution never grants Service Provider approval or category authorization.

## Inspection handoff rule

After this or any future Growth change is merged into `main` and deployed:

Report to Project Owner:
1. PR number and merge SHA.
2. Exact feature/copy/visual upgrade.
3. Exact files/routes/screens changed.
4. Deployment status and deployed SHA.
5. Direct inspection URL.
6. Exact in-app navigation path, for example:
   `Profile -> Service Provider -> Promotion Center`.
7. Anything still disabled, preview-only or approval-gated.

Never report an app upgrade as live until the deployed commit is healthy.

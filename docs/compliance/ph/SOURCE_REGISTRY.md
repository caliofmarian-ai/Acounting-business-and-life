# Philippines Official Compliance Source Registry

Verified on: 2026-09-18

A source being verified does not mean every requirement in that source applies to every Business & Life activity.

## Status meanings

- SOURCE_VERIFIED — official source identity/content verified.
- APPLICABILITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED — official source, but applicability to a specific Business & Life activity must be analyzed.
- RESEARCH_REQUIRED — topic/authority identified, but no current executable legal gate may be created yet.

## PH-DTI-BNRS-001 — DTI Business Name Registration System FAQ

Authority: Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)  
URL: https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq  
Status: SOURCE_VERIFIED  
Classification: OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE

Verified points:
- DTI explains that using a business name other than the true name requires business-name registration.
- DTI explicitly states Business Name Registration alone is not sufficient to operate; a Business/Mayor's Permit is still needed.
- DTI states BN registration validity is five years.

Platform use:
- Merchant/Supplier business-onboarding source.
- Never present DTI BN registration as complete authority to operate.

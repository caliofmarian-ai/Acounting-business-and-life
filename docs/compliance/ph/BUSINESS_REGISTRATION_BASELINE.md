# Philippines Business Registration Baseline

## Purpose

Route Merchant, Supplier and platform-operating entities toward the correct registration/compliance research path.

This is not a final permit checklist for any specific business or LGU.

## Step 1 — identify the legal/entity form

### Sole proprietorship

Official starting authority:
- DTI / BNRS.

DTI states that a business using a business name other than the owner's true name must register that name and explicitly distinguishes Business Name Registration from the Business/Mayor's Permit needed to actually operate.

### Corporation / partnership

Official starting authority:
- SEC / eSPARC.

### Cooperative

Official starting authority:
- CDA.

Do not label a local-producer group a registered cooperative until CDA registration evidence exists.

## Step 2 — BIR / tax registration

Official starting authority:
- Bureau of Internal Revenue.

Business & Life may store:
- registration status;
- reference;
- issue/effective dates;
- private evidence;
- filing/payment evidence later.

Bookkeeping in the app does not prove a government filing or tax payment.

## Step 3 — LGU Business/Mayor's Permit

Business/Mayor's Permit is local-government scoped.

Business & Life must resolve the applicable:
- city/municipality;
- BPLO/BOSS/eBOSS process;
- business activity;
- local inspections/clearances;
- forms;
- fees;
- renewal cycle.

One national hard-coded local-permit checklist is prohibited.

## Step 4 — activity-specific requirements

After entity/BIR/LGU routing, determine sector requirements.

Examples requiring separate research:
- food preparation/sale;
- regulated consumer products;
- professional services;
- transport/delivery activity;
- health/safety-regulated activity;
- employer obligations.

## Suggested evidence states

- not_configured
- research_required
- not_applicable
- application_pending
- submitted
- issued
- verified
- expired
- suspended
- revoked
- needs_review

## No-false-authorization examples

The platform must preserve these distinctions:

- DTI BN certificate uploaded is not the same as authorized to operate.
- BIR registration recorded is not the same as taxes filed.
- LGU permit application submitted is not the same as permit issued.
- Business & Life Merchant approval is not the same as government business authorization.

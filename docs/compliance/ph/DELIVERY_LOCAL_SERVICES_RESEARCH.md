# Philippines Delivery and Local Services Compliance Research Notes

## Delivery — verified source families

LTO's official LTMS is a relevant source for:
- driver's licensing;
- motor-vehicle registration;
- driving and road-safety information.

For motorized Business & Life Delivery, LTO evidence is therefore a relevant compliance source family.

## LTFRB boundary

LTFRB regulates public land transportation services and operates CPC/franchise processes.

That fact alone does not establish that every app-based goods-delivery Courier requires an LTFRB CPC/franchise.

Current Business & Life status:

LTFRB applicability to delivery-only service model = RESEARCH_REQUIRED.

Before creating an LTFRB gate, research must define:
- goods-only versus passenger transport;
- own-account versus for-hire service;
- vehicle class;
- operator/entity structure;
- compensation model;
- territory;
- applicable DOTr/LTO/LTFRB issuances.

## Vehicle-class requirement sets

Business & Life should support separate requirement sets for:
- bicycle;
- local tricycle/other local modes where lawful;
- motorcycle;
- car;
- van;
- future drone/other modes only after aviation/regulatory analysis.

## Local Services — PRC

PRC offers official professional-license verification.

Require PRC evidence only when:
- the service maps to an actually regulated profession;
- the applicable legal/professional requirement has been verified.

Do not require PRC simply because a service is skilled.

## Local Services — TESDA

TESDA Training Regulations define competency standards and assessment/certification arrangements for specific qualifications.

Possible Business & Life uses:
- relevant competency evidence;
- recommended training;
- platform risk-based credential gate;
- legally required evidence only if the legal basis is separately verified.

TESDA qualification must not be presented as a universal licence to practice.

## Category mapping statuses

Each Local Services category should eventually be classified as one of:
- UNMAPPED
- RESEARCHING
- PLATFORM_ONLY_GATE
- TESDA_RELEVANT
- PRC_REGULATED
- OTHER_REGULATOR
- NO_SPECIAL_CREDENTIAL_IDENTIFIED
- LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Credential evidence model

Store privately where appropriate:
- credential type;
- title;
- issuer;
- licence/reference number;
- issue date;
- expiry;
- evidence file;
- verification status;
- verification source/date.

Public display must remain purpose-limited.

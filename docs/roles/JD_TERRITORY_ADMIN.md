# Job Description — Territory Admin

## Role purpose

Run a local Business & Life operating cell/territory safely, consistently and transparently.

## Reporting / authority relationship

Reports to Country Admin. Authority is restricted to assigned territory and delegated permissions.

## Core responsibilities

- Onboard approved local participants.
- Perform delegated profile/application review.
- Coordinate local support/incidents.
- Track local compliance/expiry evidence.
- Maintain Merchant/Courier/service capacity visibility.
- Coordinate local authority/partner follow-up.
- Escalate exceptions.

## Permitted actions

- Use territory-scoped approvals and support tools granted by RBAC.
- Request evidence.
- Suspend/reactivate within delegated authority and documented reasons.

## Prohibited actions

- Approve outside assigned territory.
- Waive required evidence without authority.
- Accept gifts/favors that compromise decisions.
- Use incident/evidence data for unrelated purposes.

## Daily / active-case duties

- Review pending local applications.
- Monitor support/incidents.
- Check participant availability/capacity.
- Record local authority/partner actions.

## Periodic duties

- Review expiring evidence.
- Review territory KPIs.
- Review local training/compliance gaps.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Keep private documents/evidence restricted.
- Avoid sharing personal identifiers in informal channels.

## Money / financial handling

- Do not manually declare payments/settlements without evidence.
- Escalate cash/payment disputes appropriately.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Apply country + local/LGU requirement packs.
- Do not improvise legal requirements.

## Required training

- Territory operations.
- Profile approval.
- Incident handling.
- Evidence review.
- Authority communication.

## Required evidence / records

- Decision reason/evidence.
- Meeting/contact log.
- Territory readiness checklist.
- Escalations.

## Escalation

- Ambiguous compliance → Country Admin/Reviewer.
- Severe incident → Super Admin/Trust & Safety.
- System/security → technical/security operations.

## KPI framework

- Application turnaround quality.
- Local incident resolution.
- Participant availability.
- Compliance expiry backlog.
- Territory economic-loop reliability.

## Required acknowledgements

- Territory Admin agreement.
- Confidentiality/privacy.
- Conflict-of-interest.
- Local operating SOPs.

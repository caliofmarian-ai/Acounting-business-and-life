# Job Description — Country Admin

## Role purpose

Operate one Business & Life country edition consistently with global platform policy and local legal/compliance requirements.

## Reporting / authority relationship

Reports to Super Admin / Executive. Authority is limited to the assigned country edition and delegated global policy.

## Core responsibilities

- Coordinate country operations and Territory Admins.
- Maintain country legal/compliance source packs with reviewers.
- Coordinate country partnerships/providers.
- Oversee country participant approval policies.
- Manage country-level escalations.
- Ensure country documentation/localization remains current.

## Permitted actions

- Exercise country-scoped admin permissions granted by RBAC.
- Recommend country policy/configuration changes.
- Approve delegated country documents/checklists.

## Prohibited actions

- Apply one territory's local rules as universal without verification.
- Change protected global fee/security policy without authority.
- Claim government approval that has not been received.

## Daily / active-case duties

- Review country queues/escalations.
- Support Territory Admins.
- Track credential/compliance issues.

## Periodic duties

- Review official-source changes.
- Review country KPIs.
- Review territory readiness and partner performance.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Protect country participant data and evidence.
- Use private regulatory/business identifiers only for authorized tasks.

## Money / financial handling

- Coordinate country finance/settlement evidence but do not invent transaction outcomes.
- Escalate unreconciled money issues.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Ensure country rules are sourced from appropriate official authorities.
- Distinguish national from local/LGU requirements.

## Required training

- Country legal/compliance orientation.
- Admin/RBAC.
- Authority relations.
- Incident/privacy.
- Partner due diligence.

## Required evidence / records

- Country source registry.
- Territory decisions.
- Partner/authority contact logs.
- Country approvals.

## Escalation

- National legal uncertainty → qualified adviser/Executive.
- Territory conflict → Super Admin/Executive.
- Payment/provider incident → finance/security.

## KPI framework

- Territory operational health.
- Compliance review currency.
- Queue age.
- Partner readiness.
- Country incident trends.

## Required acknowledgements

- Country Admin authority agreement.
- Confidentiality/privacy.
- Conflict-of-interest.
- Country operating manual.

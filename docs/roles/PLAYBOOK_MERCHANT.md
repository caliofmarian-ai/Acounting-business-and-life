# Profile Playbook — Merchant

## Purpose

Operate an approved local business storefront and business workspace with accurate orders, stock and financial records.

> This is an operational profile playbook. It does **not** determine or imply employment, contractor, agency, franchise, partnership or worker status.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate business/storefront information.
- Record real money movements accurately.
- Process orders using allowed states/gates.
- Maintain stock/product information.
- Use accepted Supplier relationships for procurement.
- Protect Customer/private business data.
- Maintain required business registrations/permits.

## Permitted actions

- Publish approved storefront/products.
- Process Customer orders.
- Record payments/credit.
- Manage accounting/inventory/recipes.
- Invite/connect approved Suppliers.
- Create/receive/pay POs.
- Request Delivery for ready/paid orders.

## Prohibited actions

- Fabricate sales/payments.
- Mark unpaid money paid.
- Bypass presence/payment gates improperly.
- Access another business ledger.
- Duplicate linked accounting entries.
- Publish illegal/prohibited goods.
- Represent platform approval as government permit.

## Normal operating workflow

1. Confirm active workspace.
2. Review orders.
3. Process payment/presence/preparation/ready/complete states.
4. Reconcile cash/stock exceptions.
5. Review Supplier needs.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Protect Customer contact/order data.
- Restrict business financial records to authorized staff.

## Money / commercial handling

- Use Cash/GCash/Bank/Other accurately.
- Record actual receipts/payments.
- Track receivables/payables.
- Close/reconcile cash.
- Never duplicate linked financial entries.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Business owner remains responsible for registrations, tax and local permits.
- Use current PH/LGU compliance checklist once SOURCE_VERIFIED.

## Training / competency

- Merchant onboarding.
- Accounting/cash.
- Orders/stock.
- Procurement.
- Delivery dispatch.
- Incident/privacy.

## Evidence / documents

- Business registration/permit evidence as applicable.
- Business workspace authorization.
- Storefront/catalog records.
- Accounting evidence.

## Escalation

- Money discrepancy → Finance/Admin.
- Compliance uncertainty → Reviewer/Country Admin.
- Safety/fraud → Incident.
- System problem → Support.

## KPI / quality indicators

- Order completion.
- Cash variance.
- Stock accuracy.
- Receivable exposure.
- Supplier fulfilment quality.
- Incident/compliance status.

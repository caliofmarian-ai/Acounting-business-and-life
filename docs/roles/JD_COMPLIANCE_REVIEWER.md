# Job Description — Compliance / Credential Reviewer

## Role purpose

Review submitted business/profile evidence consistently against current country/territory/category requirements.

## Reporting / authority relationship

Reports to Country Admin / designated Compliance lead. Decisions are limited to documented requirement checklists and delegated authority.

## Core responsibilities

- Identify applicable requirement checklist/version.
- Review evidence type, issuer/reference/dates and expiry.
- Record verification basis and reason.
- Request missing/corrected evidence.
- Distinguish platform verification from government issuance.
- Escalate ambiguity.

## Permitted actions

- Mark evidence status within authorized categories.
- Request resubmission.
- Recommend suspension for expired/invalid evidence according to policy.

## Prohibited actions

- Invent legal requirements.
- Verify evidence solely because it looks plausible.
- Alter evidence.
- Represent platform verification as official government certification.

## Daily / active-case duties

- Review assigned queue.
- Record reasons.
- Check source/version.
- Protect evidence.

## Periodic duties

- Review stale legal/source packs.
- Audit consistency.
- Complete category-specific updates.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Evidence is private by default.
- Do not expose identity/licence numbers publicly unless specifically authorized and necessary.

## Money / financial handling

- Do not infer payment/fee compliance from unrelated financial data.
- Escalate tax/payment evidence questions.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Use official-source/current controlled requirements.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly.
- Seek specialist review when necessary.

## Required training

- Evidence verification.
- Source registry.
- Fraud/red-flag awareness.
- Privacy.
- Role/category-specific requirements.

## Required evidence / records

- Evidence metadata.
- Decision/reason.
- Source/checklist version.
- Expiry/review date.

## Escalation

- Uncertain authenticity → enhanced review/Admin.
- Legal ambiguity → Country Admin/qualified adviser.
- Suspected fraud → Trust & Safety.

## KPI framework

- Decision consistency.
- Reversal/error rate.
- Queue age.
- Expired-evidence backlog.
- Source/version compliance.

## Required acknowledgements

- Reviewer ethics/conflict-of-interest.
- Privacy/confidentiality.
- Verification standard.
- No-false-approval rule.

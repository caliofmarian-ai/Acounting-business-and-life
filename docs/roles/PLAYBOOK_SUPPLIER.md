# Profile Playbook — Supplier

## Purpose

Supply approved Merchant businesses through accurate catalog data, confirmed purchase orders, fulfilment and business accounting.

> This is an operational profile playbook. It does **not** determine or imply employment, contractor, agency, franchise, partnership or worker status.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate catalog/availability/lead time.
- Accept only relationships/POs you intend to serve.
- Confirm quantities/prices honestly.
- Fulfil and communicate changes.
- Maintain own business compliance and financial records.

## Permitted actions

- Accept/revoke Merchant relationships.
- Manage catalog.
- Accept/partially accept/reject POs.
- Update fulfilment status.
- Use Supplier accounting workspace.

## Prohibited actions

- Change historical PO snapshots.
- Claim delivery/receipt/payment that did not occur.
- Access Merchant private accounting.
- Misrepresent licences/registration.

## Normal operating workflow

1. Review incoming relationships/POs.
2. Confirm availability/prices/ETA.
3. Prepare/dispatch/pickup.
4. Review receivables/payment records.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Protect Merchant commercial information and private account data.

## Money / commercial handling

- Supplier-side accounting records actual linked receipts.
- Do not duplicate PO receipts manually.
- Track receivables.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Supplier business remains responsible for applicable registrations/tax/product rules.
- Use category/jurisdiction checklist.

## Training / competency

- Catalog/PO workflow.
- Receiving/payment implications.
- Accounting.
- Compliance/privacy.

## Evidence / documents

- Business/profile evidence.
- Catalog records.
- PO fulfilment records.
- Registration/product compliance evidence where required.

## Escalation

- PO dispute → Merchant/Support.
- Money discrepancy → Finance/Admin.
- Compliance → Reviewer/Country Admin.
- Incident → private report.

## KPI / quality indicators

- PO acceptance/fulfilment.
- Partial/failed fulfilment.
- Lead-time accuracy.
- Receivable aging.
- Compliance status.

## Required acknowledgements

- Supplier terms.
- Catalog accuracy.
- Privacy.
- Compliance responsibility.

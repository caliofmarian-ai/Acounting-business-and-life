# Profile Playbook — Customer

## Purpose

Use Business & Life to discover local Merchants/Service Providers, place orders/requests, track fulfilment and report problems responsibly.

> This is an operational profile playbook. It does **not** determine or imply employment, contractor, agency, franchise, partnership or worker status.

## Responsibilities

- Provide accurate account/contact information.
- Use orders/requests in good faith.
- Pay amounts legitimately owed.
- Protect handoff/security codes.
- Confirm service completion truthfully.
- Use incident/review tools appropriately.

## Permitted actions

- Browse published storefronts/providers.
- Place supported orders.
- Check in for cash pickup when required.
- Track own orders/deliveries.
- Request services.
- Submit reviews only when eligible.
- Submit private incidents.

## Prohibited actions

- Fraudulent orders/chargebacks/evidence.
- Sharing another person's private data without reason.
- Misusing handoff codes.
- Posting incident evidence as public harassment.
- Attempting unauthorized business/admin access.

## Normal operating workflow

1. Choose Merchant/provider.
2. Review terms/options.
3. Place order/request.
4. Follow status/payment requirements.
5. Receive/confirm.
6. Review/report if appropriate.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Keep account credentials private.
- Only submit necessary incident evidence.
- Do not publish another person's personal data.

## Money / commercial handling

- Use supported payment options.
- Do not claim payment occurred unless it did.
- Resolve outstanding balances through the proper workflow.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Follow applicable public terms and lawful use requirements.
- Service/product legality remains subject to local rules.

## Training / competency

- Account/security basics.
- Order/payment/delivery status basics.
- Incident/review distinction.

## Evidence / documents

- Account identity/contact.
- Order/service request records.
- Optional incident evidence.

## Escalation

- Account/security problem → Support.
- Money dispute → Support/Finance path.
- Safety/fraud → private Incident.
- Service dispute → job/incident workflow.

## KPI / quality indicators

- Legitimate completion rate.
- Low preventable cancellation/abuse.
- Accurate confirmations/reviews.

## Required acknowledgements

- Terms/privacy notice.
- Acceptable-use rules.
- Payment/order responsibilities.

# Job Description — Project Owner / Executive

## Role purpose

Own the mission, strategic direction, protected commercial decisions and overall governance of Business & Life.

## Reporting / authority relationship

Highest strategic authority. May delegate operating authority but retains ownership of protected strategic decisions.

## Core responsibilities

- Approve mission, vision and strategic scope.
- Approve country entry/exit and major territory strategy.
- Approve protected fee/monetization policies.
- Appoint or remove senior delegated authorities.
- Approve major partnerships and executive-confidential governance.
- Resolve material conflicts between product, operations, finance and compliance.
- Ensure the company has a documented operating model rather than relying on informal memory.

## Permitted actions

- Access all authorized executive/business records.
- Approve or reject strategic proposals.
- Delegate defined authority in writing/system records.
- Suspend strategic initiatives where risk is unacceptable.

## Prohibited actions

- Represent unverified legal claims as confirmed law.
- Bypass financial/security controls for convenience.
- Issue hidden operational rules outside controlled documentation when they affect user rights or money.
- Use private evidence for unrelated purposes.

## Daily / active-case duties

- Review material blockers and escalations.
- Make owner decisions explicitly when required.
- Review major incidents/financial anomalies that exceed delegated authority.

## Periodic duties

- Review masterplan and risk register.
- Review country/territory readiness.
- Review commercial model and major partnerships.
- Review executive KPIs and governance exceptions.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Treat executive, personal, business and authority information according to access purpose.
- Avoid unnecessary exposure of private user/business evidence.

## Money / financial handling

- Approve financial policy, but do not fabricate accounting/payment evidence.
- Require reconciliation and auditability for money movement.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Require official-source verification for material legal/compliance claims.
- Obtain professional advice where risk/complexity warrants it.

## Required training

- Governance and delegated-authority training.
- Security/privacy briefing.
- Financial control and conflict-of-interest training.
- Country-specific compliance briefing before major country decisions.

## Required evidence / records

- Owner decisions / approvals.
- Delegation records.
- Risk acceptance records.
- Major partnership approvals.
- Strategic change log.

## Escalation

- Severe security/privacy incident → specialist/legal response.
- Material regulatory uncertainty → qualified legal/compliance advice.
- Material finance discrepancy → finance/reconciliation investigation.

## KPI framework

- Pilot readiness and economic-loop reliability.
- Strategic milestones completed with evidence.
- Material risk closure.
- Country/territory performance after launch.
- Documented delegation and governance quality.

## Required acknowledgements

- Code of conduct.
- Confidentiality/privacy obligations.
- Conflict-of-interest declaration.
- Executive governance standard.

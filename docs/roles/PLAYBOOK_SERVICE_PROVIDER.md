# Profile Playbook — Service Provider / Local Services

## Purpose

Offer approved skills/services through a transparent request, quote, job, completion and verified-review workflow.

> This is an operational profile playbook. It does **not** determine or imply employment, contractor, agency, franchise, partnership or worker status.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate public profile/services.
- Submit truthful credentials/evidence.
- Quote clearly.
- Perform accepted work professionally/safely.
- Respect property/privacy.
- Complete job status truthfully.

## Permitted actions

- Publish profile within approved visibility/scope.
- Select services.
- Submit credentials/portfolio.
- Receive requests.
- Quote.
- Schedule/work.
- Complete jobs.
- Receive verified reviews after Customer confirmation.

## Prohibited actions

- Offer prohibited/unauthorized regulated work.
- Falsify credentials/experience/reviews.
- Publish Customer job images without required consent.
- Mark unperformed work completed.
- Use private Customer data beyond job purpose.

## Normal operating workflow

1. Review requests.
2. Clarify scope.
3. Quote.
4. Wait for acceptance.
5. Schedule.
6. Perform.
7. Complete.
8. Resolve issues.
9. Maintain profile/credentials.

## Privacy / confidentiality

- Private CV/evidence remains private.
- Customer publication consent governs job-linked portfolio.
- Use Customer address/contact only for service purpose.

## Money / commercial handling

- Quote/final price must be clear.
- Do not fabricate payment status.
- Future platform fees/payment settlement follow approved policy only.

## Legal / compliance boundary

- Professional/category requirements vary by service and jurisdiction; follow current verified checklist.
- Platform verification does not replace government/professional authorization.

## Training / competency

- Profile/privacy.
- Quoting/jobs.
- Safety/property care.
- Credentials/compliance.
- Incident/dispute.

## Evidence / documents

- Credentials/licences/training where applicable.
- Portfolio consent.
- Job records.
- Application/authorization.

## Escalation

- Scope/legal ambiguity → Reviewer/Admin.
- Safety/dispute → Incident.
- Money → Support/Finance.
- App → Support.

## KPI / quality indicators

- Quote acceptance.
- Job completion.
- Customer confirmation.
- Verified reviews.
- Dispute rate.
- Credential currency.

## Required acknowledgements

- Service Provider terms.
- Professional conduct.
- Privacy/portfolio consent.
- Credential truthfulness.
- Compliance responsibility.

## Applicable SOP placeholders

- profile onboarding/authorization SOP;
- privacy and evidence SOP;
- incident/reporting SOP;
- territory-specific compliance checklist;
- task-specific workflow SOPs.

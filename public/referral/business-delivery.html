BUSINESS DELIVERY
FOR RESTAURANTS, FAST-FOOD & LOCAL SHOPS

Need a courier for an order?

For eligible Business & Life customer orders, the app can coordinate an approved Courier pickup, delivery status and secure handoff while keeping delivery money separate from merchandise revenue.

I RUN A BUSINESS

You have the order. Coordinate the Courier.

Delivery quote → payment/readiness gates → approved Courier assignment → Merchant pickup → in transit → secure customer handoff.

I WANT TO DELIVER

Become an approved Courier.

Approved Couriers can publish availability and receive eligible delivery assignments within vehicle/capacity/radius rules.

From order to delivered.

1. Customer chooses Delivery
2. Delivery quote uses active pricing rules
3. Payment/readiness gates pass
4. Approved Courier assigned
5. Courier arrives & picks up
6. Track → arrival → secure handoff
Current boundary: this service is for eligible Business & Life orders. It does not yet mean “send any external parcel.” Courier availability is not guaranteed, prices are not invented, and cash delivery remains disabled where current policy blocks it.
Future extension: Standalone Business Courier Booking — allowing a Merchant to request a Courier for an external/off-platform order — is a separate product extension and is not live yet.
